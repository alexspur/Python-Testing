HOW THE C225 OUTPUT TRACE IS BUILT
==================================

Shots 43, 44, 45, 46, session experiment_log_20260924_170806 (24 Sept 2026).
Signal: scope 3 (Rigol DS7054) CH4 = C225 D-dot, probe FC032.
Pipeline: analysis/pipeline.py, pipeline_version "py-2".

Line numbers refer to the copies in the source/ folder of this export.


1. WHICH CODE MAKES THE TRACE
-----------------------------

There is one C225 trace and it is made in Python.

  source/ScopeDelayGUI/analysis/process_shots.py
      process_one(), line 166. Reads the three rigol CSV files (line 187)
      and calls pipeline.process_waveforms() (line 205). save_mat() (line 237,
      function at line 257) writes logs/processed_shots/shot_NNNN.mat.
      PIPELINE_VERSION = "py-2" is line 42.

  source/ScopeDelayGUI/analysis/pipeline.py
      process_waveforms(), line 227. The C225 block is lines 287-311.
      It stores S["C225_t"] (line 309) and S["C225"] (line 310).

  source/logs/ltgs_gui3.m  (the MATLAB GUI)
      Does NOT compute anything for C225. loadAll() (line 604) loads the
      shot_*.mat files written by process_shots.py, and getTrace() (lines
      728-729) plots S.C225_t against S.C225 as they are. No filter, shift or
      baseline is applied in ltgs_gui3.m.

  source/process_shots.m  (the older MATLAB pipeline)
      process_waveforms(), line 417, C225 block lines 498-508. Same math as
      pipeline.py (pipeline.py is a line-by-line port of it). It is NOT what
      made the .mat files for these shots: those carry pipeline_version py-2.
      The only C225 difference: MATLAB takes the sample interval as
      t(2)-t(1) (lines 503 and 655); Python takes it over the whole record
      (sample_dt, pipeline.py line 108). Same value on a clean record.

  source/logs/shot_timing.py
      The 50% edges, pulse widths and gap are found here and only here.
      It builds its OWN, DIFFERENT C225 output trace for that (section 9).
      Neither pipeline.py, process_shots.py, process_shots.m nor ltgs_gui3.m
      finds 50% edges. A comment in shot_timing.py (line 72-73, 333) says
      out_edges() is a port of "outEdges in ltgs_gui3.m", but no function of
      that name exists in ltgs_gui3.m or anywhere else in the project.

Checked for these four shots: the trace rebuilt step by step by
make_export.py, the output of process_waveforms(), and the C225 stored in
logs/processed_shots/shot_00NN.mat are identical (max difference 0 kV, time
axis difference 0, tPulse difference 0).


2. STEPS IN ORDER (pipeline.py)
-------------------------------

Step 1. Read the file.  read_rigol(), lines 120-135.
    rigol3_20260924_170806_shotNN.csv, column 0 = time (s), column 4 = CH4 (V).
    Rows with a non-finite time are dropped. Nothing else.
    Record: 1,000,000 samples, 0.4 ns apart, about -200 to +200 us around
    pulse 1. CH4 is 8-bit: one code step is 1.3568 V in the exported volts.

Step 2. Find tPulse and tEnd.  process_waveforms() lines 237-247, using
    detect_activity(), lines 174-188.
    detect_activity on each of three "anchor" channels:
        scope 2 CH3 (LTGS1-232 D-dot), scope 2 CH4 (LTGS1-007 B-dot),
        scope 3 CH4 (C225 D-dot).
    detect_activity(t, v), defaults k = 10, env_s = 200 ns, noise_s = 500 ns:
        v0 = v - median(v)
        noise: split v0 into blocks of 500 ns (1250 samples), take the std of
        each block (ddof 1), sort, sigma = the 25th-percentile block std
        env = moving max of |v0| over 200 ns (500 samples, movmax line 58)
        above = env > 10 * sigma
        start = first time above, end = last time above.
    tPulse = median of the three starts, tEnd = max of the three ends.
    These are scope record times. Scope 2 and scope 3 record times are mixed
    here with no cable or trigger correction.
    Values: see edges.csv, tPulse_record_s and pulse_window_us_end
    (tEnd - tPulse: 1.670, 1.219, 1.121, 0.940 us for shots 43-46).

Step 3. Time axis. NO time shift or cable delay is applied to C225.
    t_pipeline_us = (t_record - tPulse) * 1e6     (line 309)
    So t = 0 is tPulse from step 2, not the C225 edge itself. On these shots
    the C225 50% start of pulse 1 lands at about +0.161 to +0.163 us.

Step 4. Subtract the pre-pulse mean of the raw signal.  lines 257, 293, 304.
    r3_pre = (max(tPulse - 2 us, t3[0] + 0.1 us), tPulse - 0.3 us)
    open interval (inwin(), line 103: t > a and t < b)
    v = CH4 - mean(CH4 over r3_pre)
    Window -2.000 to -0.300 us on all four shots. premean_v in edges.csv.

Step 5. Activity mask, the samples left OUT of the baseline fit.
    lines 305-307.
    act = "above" from detect_activity(t3, v) on the step-4 signal, same
    parameters as step 2, run over the WHOLE scope 3 record.
    Then widened by 0.3 us on each side: padN = round(0.3 us / dt) = 750
    samples, act = movmax(act, 2*padN + 1) > 0.
    Result on these shots: one interval each, about -0.25 us to tEnd + 0.3 us
    (shot 43: -0.246 to 1.970 us). 3,669 to 5,541 samples excluded out of
    1,000,000. Exact intervals: edges.csv, excluded_intervals_us.

Step 6. Filtering or smoothing of the signal: NONE.
    The only moving windows in the C225 path are inside detect_activity
    (200 ns moving max, used only to build the mask) and the 1501-sample
    moving max that widens the mask. The trace itself is never smoothed.

Step 7. Offset step removal or piecewise correction: NONE for C225.
    remove_offset_step() (line 191) is used only for the rigol2 D-dots and
    B-dots, not for C225.

Step 8. Integrate.  reconstruct_quiet(), line 211, cumtrapz() line 91.
    integ = cumulative trapezoid of v (step 4) against t in seconds,
    scipy cumulative_trapezoid with initial = 0.
    Starts at the FIRST sample of the record (about -200 us), not at the
    pulse. Units V*s. Nothing else is subtracted before integrating.

Step 9. Baseline: cubic fit on the INTEGRATED trace.  reconstruct_quiet(),
    lines 212-214, polyfit_mu() lines 95-100.
    Fit samples: every sample where act is False, over the WHOLE record
    (about -200 to +200 us). Only the step-5 interval is left out.
    Fit variable: x = (t_record - mu0) / mu1, t_record in SECONDS (record
    time, not t_pipeline), mu0 = mean of t over the fit samples,
    mu1 = std of t over the fit samples (ddof 1, about 115.7 us). This is
    MATLAB's [p, S, mu] = polyfit(t, y, 3) centring and scaling.
    Model: integ ~ p3 x^3 + p2 x^2 + p1 x + p0 (numpy.polyfit, ordinary least
    squares, all fit samples weighted equally).
    baseline(t) = polyval(p, (t - mu0) / mu1), units V*s.
    integ = integ - baseline.
    Coefficients, mu0, mu1: edges.csv (fit_p3..fit_p0, fit_mu0_record_s,
    fit_mu1_s).

Step 10. Zero the trace just before the pulse.  reconstruct_quiet(), line 215.
    c3_zero = (max(tPulse - 0.9 us, t3[0]), tPulse - 0.1 us), line 258,
    open interval.
    integ = integ - mean(integ over c3_zero)    (one constant, V*s)
    Window -0.900 to -0.100 us on all four shots. zero_offset_Vs in edges.csv.

Step 11. Calibrate to kV.  reconstruct_quiet() line 216, process_waveforms()
    lines 308 and 310.
    C225 [V] = CF3_CH4 * integ,   CF3_CH4 = 1.56e11 V per V*s  (line 32)
    S["C225"] = C225 / 1e3   -> kV.
    Source given in the code (lines 19-23): Multipulse_Calibrations_and_
    Connections.xlsx, "Calibration Matrix" and "Scope Connections" sheets,
    L-3 certificates 2019, probe FC032. The 20 dB pad is already in the scope
    probe ratio (x10), so the exported volts include it. The sign is kept:
    the output pulses are negative (about -195 to -211 kV peak).

Step 12. Peak value.  line 311.
    peaks["C225_Ddot"] = max |C225| over the WHOLE record, in kV.
    This is the C225_Ddot_kV column of processed_shots/shot_summary.csv.
    WARNING: on these shots that maximum is NOT the output pulse. It is the
    leftover baseline drift far from the pulse (see section 3). Summary values
    839, 834, 753, 499 kV for shots 43-46, while the pulses are about
    195-211 kV.


3. WHAT THE BASELINE DOES ON THESE SHOTS
----------------------------------------

The integral of the raw signal wanders slowly over the 400 us record
(panel 2 of the plots, peak to peak about 1.2e-5 V*s). One cubic over the
whole record cannot follow that wander, so after step 9 the trace still
carries slow drift, and only step 10 makes it look flat near the pulse:

  shot   whole-record C225 range    -3 to -0.3 us before pulse 1
                                    mean     slope       std
  43       -195 ..  839 kV          -5.3     +6.8 kV/us   5.6 kV
  44       -834 ..   48 kV         +17.2    -13.2 kV/us  10.5 kV
  45       -753 ..  130 kV         +15.4    -13.5 kV/us  10.7 kV
  46       -265 ..  499 kV          +4.6     -5.3 kV/us   4.3 kV

1.56e11 V/(V*s) turns 1e-6 V*s of drift into 156 kV, so a baseline error of
1e-8 V*s is already 1.6 kV.


4. HOW THE PULSE START AND WINDOW ARE FOUND
-------------------------------------------

tPulse and tEnd: step 2. The "pulse window" used for C225 is the step-5
activity mask (detect_activity on scope 3 CH4 alone, padded 0.3 us), not
tPulse..tEnd directly. tEnd is not used in the C225 path at all.


5. 50% EDGES, WIDTH, GAP  (shot_timing.py only)
-----------------------------------------------

Not done in the pipeline, the .mat files or ltgs_gui3.m. shot_timing.py does
it on its own trace:

  Time axis: sensor time after T0, in ns, lines 815-816:
      t_sensor = t_record*1e9 - record_to_sensor(3, 4)
      record_to_sensor (line 128) = cable one way - trigger arrival - scope
      offset = 149.672/2 - 18.391 - (-87.138) = 143.583 ns for these shots
      (old trigger cables, before 5 Oct 2026; use_trigger_cables, line 114).
  First C225 burst, lines 820-842 and find_bursts() line 279: in the window
      TG-100 trigger in (map prediction, predict() line 200, Marx delay
      180 us default) -2000 to +12000 ns; baseline = median of samples before
      TG-100 in - 200 ns; first crossing of 30% of the largest |deviation|.
  Its output trace, output_trace() line 315, called at line 862:
      raw CH4 from 2000 ns before to 3500 ns after that first burst
      minus the mean of the raw samples from 2000 ns to 150 ns before it
      (a constant, no fit), then cumsum * dt (rectangle rule, dt = median
      sample spacing). No calibration factor, no cubic.
  Edges, out_edges() line 331, called at line 864 with frac = 0.5:
      5 ns centred moving mean (movmean, line 303; 13 samples at 0.4 ns),
      flip the sign so the largest peak is positive, pulses = runs above 50%
      of the largest peak that last 30 ns or more, first two runs used.
      For each pulse, level = 50% of THAT pulse's own peak. Start = last
      sample below the level before the pulse's 50% region, linearly
      interpolated. End = last sample at or above the level inside the run,
      interpolated to the next sample.
  width = end - start, gap = pulse 2 start - pulse 1 end,
  start to start = pulse 2 start - pulse 1 start (lines 941-958).

Because a 50% level on each pulse's own peak is insensitive to a constant
offset, both traces give edges that agree to about 0.01-0.35 ns at the
pulse 1 start. Widths and gaps differ by up to about 2.6 ns (shot 44); see
edges.csv. make_export.py reproduces the saved
timing_summary_20260924_170806_shots_43_to_46.csv widths and gaps exactly.


6. THE EXPORTED FILES
---------------------

shot_00NN_c225.csv   every sample from t_pipeline = -3 us to +4 us (17,500 rows)
shot_00NN_c225_whole_record.csv   every 100th sample of the whole record

  t_record_ns           scope 3 record time, ns
  t_pipeline_us         (t_record - tPulse)*1e6, the axis of S.C225_t
  raw_v                 raw CH4, scope volts (exported volts, pad included)
  baseline              the cubic of step 9, V*s. It is subtracted from
                        integral_before, so it is in the same units.
  integral_before       cumtrapz of (raw_v - pre-pulse mean) from the first
                        record sample, V*s, before the cubic is removed.
                        Note: the pre-pulse mean (step 4) is already removed
                        from the integrand.
  c225_kv               the final trace, = S.C225 in the .mat file, kV
  -- extra columns, because the steps differ from the columns asked for --
  integrand_v           raw_v - pre-pulse mean, the signal actually integrated
  baseline_raw_equiv_v  pre-pulse mean + d/dt of the cubic, V. The same
                        correction expressed in raw scope volts. This is what
                        panel 1 of the plots draws on the raw signal.
  in_fit                1 if the sample is used in the cubic fit, 0 if it is
                        in the excluded activity interval

  To rebuild c225_kv from the columns:
      c225_kv = 1.56e11 * (integral_before - baseline - zero_offset_Vs) / 1e3
  with zero_offset_Vs from edges.csv.

edges.csv   one row per shot
  tPulse_record_s, tEnd_record_s       step 2, record seconds
  pulse_window_us_start/_end           0 and tEnd - tPulse, pipeline us
  premean_window_us, premean_v         step 4
  zero_window_us, zero_offset_Vs       step 10
  fit_p3..fit_p0                       cubic coefficients, highest power first,
                                       in V*s, of x = (t_record_s - mu0)/mu1
  fit_mu0_record_s, fit_mu1_s          centring and scaling of x
  fit_range_us                         first to last fit sample, pipeline us
  fit_samples_used / _excluded, excluded_intervals_us   step 5
  CF_V_per_Vs                          step 11
  check_max_diff_vs_saved_mat_kV       0 means the export equals the .mat
  pl_*   50% edges from shot_timing.out_edges() run unchanged on the
         PIPELINE C225 trace, in the window -2 to +3.5 us. Times in pipeline us,
         widths and gaps in ns, peaks in kV.
  st_*   50% edges from shot_timing.py's own trace (section 5), recomputed
         with its functions and moved to the pipeline time axis
         (t_record = t_sensor + 143.583 ns, then minus tPulse). Peaks in its
         own uncalibrated units (V*s).
  saved_timing_summary_*   the values in the saved timing summary CSV, for
         comparison with st_*.

shot_00NN_baseline.png   three panels
  1. raw CH4, -2.3 to 0 us, with the baseline in raw volts
     (baseline_raw_equiv_v) and the pre-mean window. The y range is set by
     the raw codes: CH4 moves in 1.36 V steps, so the baseline sits inside
     the quantisation noise.
  2. integral_before and the cubic baseline over the whole record, excluded
     interval shaded.
  3. c225_kv from -0.4 to +1.4 us with both sets of 50% edges.

make_export.py   the script that wrote all of the above. Read-only on the
  data. Calls the existing functions unchanged.
