WHAT CHANGED: C225 AND C315 OUTPUT TRACES, PIPELINE py-2 -> py-3
================================================================

Date: 6 Oct 2026. Session checked: logs/2026.09.24/experiment_log_20260924_170806.
Line numbers refer to the files in source/.


WHY
---
Up to py-2, C225 was the integral of the whole 400 us record minus one cubic
fitted to that integral. The cubic cannot follow the slow drift of the
integral, so near the pulse the trace kept a slope (up to 18 kV/us) and an
offset (up to 9.5 kV), and the 50% edges moved by up to 2.6 ns from shot to
shot. C315 was a linear fit on a short pre-pulse window and drifted even
more over its span. py-3 uses a baseline local to the pulse.


THE NEW METHOD (C225 and C315 the same way, each from its own raw signal)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. act = detect_activity(t3, raw), the existing function, unchanged.
2. Onset t_on: dev = |raw - median(raw)|, set to 0 where act is false,
   t_on = time of the first sample with dev >= 0.3 * max(dev).
3. Baseline = mean of raw over t_on - 2.0 us <= t < t_on - 0.15 us.
   Fewer than 100 samples there: the probe is skipped with a warning.
4. integ = trapezoid integral of (raw - baseline) over the whole record.
5. integ minus its mean over t_on - 60 ns < t < t_on - 20 ns.
6. Trace in kV = factor * integ / 1000.
   C225: factor CF3_CH4 = 1.56e11. C315: CF3_CH3 * geom * bScale.
Saved extents unchanged: C225 over the whole record, C315 from
tPulse - 0.9 us to the end of the record. Both on (t3 - tPulse) * 1e6.
Peaks are now the largest |value| inside evt_win (tPulse - 1 us to
tEnd + 1 us) only.

WHAT DID NOT GO AS ASKED, OR NEEDS A DECISION
---------------------------------------------
- logs/processed_shots/shot_summary.csv was NOT updated. Windows refused to
  replace it (Access is denied, twice), most likely because it is open in
  Excel. The new rows are in summary_cache.json, and the pipeline left them
  in logs/processed_shots/shot_summary.tmp. summary.png was not redrawn
  either. Close the file and run
      python -m analysis.process_shots --session logs/2026.09.24/experiment_log_20260924_170806 --no-plots
  from ScopeDelayGUI. Every shot is cached, so it only rewrites the summary.
  The session rows in this export were written from the cache with the
  pipeline's own write_summary().
- Skips are now per probe, with warnings "C225 skipped: ..." and
  "C315 skipped: ..." instead of one "C225/C315 skipped". A probe is also
  skipped if it shows no activity at all or the zero window holds no
  samples. Both are guards, not part of the method; neither happens on
  these shots.
- C315 kV in edge_table.csv is the pipeline value. ltgs_gui3_5.m multiplies
  C315 by -4.5/-5.5 = 0.818 when it shows it (bdotRescale), so its own
  edge table would print C315 peak and level kV 0.818 times these. Times,
  widths and gaps are not affected. The pipeline still builds C315 with
  bScale = -5.5.
- edge_table.csv has more decimals than the GUI prints (it prints widths
  to 0.1 ns), so 0.05 ns differences stay visible.
- In shot_00NN_output.csv the C315 columns are empty before -0.9 us,
  because that is where the saved C315 span starts (unchanged, as asked).
- The other copy at Downloads/ltgs_gui3.m is the older GUI. Only
  logs/ltgs_gui3_5.m has outEdges.
- PIPELINE_VERSION py-3 means every other session is redone the next time
  someone runs a full reprocess. The GUI's automatic analysis after a
  shot processes only that shot, and it runs this working copy, so the
  next shot fired is processed with py-3.
- The rest of the test folder (test_analysis_gui.py and others) does not
  reference the analysis package and was not run.
